# Video Manifest

WRO 2026 requirement (chapter 7): a public/link-access YouTube video of the vehicle **driving autonomously**, at least **30 seconds** of driving per video, **one video per challenge**. Upload before the documentation deadline; embed links in the root README.

## Checklist for every upload

- [ ] Video shows the vehicle driving autonomously (no remote control visible).
- [ ] ≥ 30 s of uninterrupted driving.
- [ ] Filename and description state: date, challenge (Open/Obstacle), track setup, outcome.
- [ ] Unedited single take preferred; if edited, no cuts inside the driving section.
- [ ] Vehicle photo (side/top) shown so judges can identify the robot.
