# Mechanical Bill of Materials

Parts list for the chassis and drivetrain. This excludes the electronics (see the [electronics guide](../electronics/README.md)). Quantities and statuses are reviewed each build; statuses marked "to confirm" are checked before the next cut.

Status legend: **in hand** (already owned), **to confirm** (quantity or spec to be confirmed), **pending** (to be sourced or modelled).

| Part | Qty | Spec | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Plywood deck sheet | 1 | 3 mm, laser-cuts cleanly | In hand |
| Brass standoffs | 4-6 | M3, ~35 mm effective spacing | In hand |
| N20 gear motor | 1 (+1 spare) | 6 V, ~600 RPM, spare carried | In hand |
| Rear wheels | 2 | ~30 mm diameter, driven axle | To confirm |
| Front wheels | 2 | ~40 mm diameter, high-traction rubber | To confirm |
| TowerPro MG996R servo | 1 (+1 spare) | Metal gear, 4.8-7.2 V | In hand |
| Steering knuckles | 2 | 3D-printed, STL pending in repo | Pending |
| Tie rod | 1 | Connects horn to knuckles | To confirm |
| LEGO beams and pins | Assorted | 8 mm grid mounting rails | In hand |
| LEGO axle and connectors | Assorted | Steering and mounting | In hand |
| Servo horn | 1 | Included with servo | In hand |
| Screws, nuts, washers | Assorted | M2/M3 | In hand |
| Cable ties and adhesive pads | Assorted | Cable management and mounting | In hand |
| Clear varnish | 1 | Two coats on cut edges | In hand |

## Notes

- The spare N20 and spare MG996R are field-repair parts carried to the event, not fitted.
- The steering knuckle STL files are the only mechanical part still to be committed to the repository; until then the geometry is defined by the LEGO sub-frame jig in the [design overview](README.md).
- Update this file when a status changes so the repository stays the single source of truth for a rebuild.
